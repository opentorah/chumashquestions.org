## Not universal

These are still Latin in the glossary, but they are Jewish English, not general loan-words: *shul, bentch, daven, milchigs, fleishigs, Shabbos, Chumash, Gemora, posuk, korban, parsha, tallis, tefillin, tzitzis, brocho, minyan, chumrahs, gebrokts,* and most other yeshivish/liturgical lemmas.

## Not in this book

English also has Yiddish loans that do not appear in the questions: *chutzpah, bagel, mensch, schlep, klutz, nosh, kvetch, spiel, yarmulke, challah, lox, kibitz, maven,* and similar.

## Display

Only the first table belongs in Latin *because they are English*. Everything else that is still Latin is a name, a holiday label kept Latin on purpose, or Jewish English—not a general loan-word.
